# First human playtest

Run `pnpm dev --port 5173`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5173/ on a desktop or laptop with a keyboard. Click the pitch to enable audio. The game opens directly into a five-minute match; leaving the window pauses it.

## One match

1. **First minute:** drive with WASD, brake/reverse with S, and boost with Shift. Try a kickoff hit, then a slow approach. Does the car go where you expect?
2. **Second minute:** switch cameras with C. Follow the ball into a corner and near both goalposts. Note any sudden camera movement or lost view of the car/ball.
3. **Third minute:** hold Space for a higher jump; release and tap it again while holding a direction to flip. Try Q/E air roll and opposite pitch during a flip. Press B for infinite boost if useful.
4. **Fourth minute:** drive onto a side wall, drop back to the pitch, and try Ctrl powerslide. Space should right an overturned car; R recovers it to kickoff position.
5. **Finish:** play for goals against Maverick. Check the goal celebration, next kickoff, final result, and Restart Match. Use P/Esc to pause; Tab reaches the menu controls and Space activates a focused button.

For a quieter mechanics check, choose **Solo practice** in the pause menu. It still uses the match clock and scoring. **Performance** graphics is available if High feels slow.

## Useful feedback

Record the most disruptive issue first:

- What you tried, keys held, and roughly where on the pitch.
- Car cam or ball cam; High or Performance graphics.
- What happened versus what you expected. A short recording is especially useful for handling and camera problems.
- Whether it repeats after R or Restart Match.

Then answer: **Would you voluntarily play another match? What is the first thing you would change?**

This candidate has automated physics, match, controls and camera checks plus browser scenario checks. Human handling feel, bot difficulty, exact original-game fidelity and other devices remain unverified. Advanced mechanics are custom approximations.
